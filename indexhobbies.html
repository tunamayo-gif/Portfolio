My Hobbies

Most of my days are consumed by the pull of video games, especially high-stakes multiplayer MOBA matches that demand sharp reflexes and team coordination, alongside deep, lore-heavy RPGs where I can lose myself in character progression and expansive worlds.

However, whenever the repetitive grind or burnout finally sets in, I naturally pivot back to what feels like a comforting old habit—diving straight into a good book.

Somewhere in the background lingers a genuine passion for drawing, a creative outlet I truly cherish, though it has unfortunately taken a backseat because capturing the ideas in my head demands a block of time and energy I rarely seem to find anymore.
